Privacy Policy

Cocoshell Labs LLC · Effective October 12, 2026

Cocoshell is built to be private. This page explains, in plain language, what we do and don't collect when you use the Cocoshell apps and service. "We" means Cocoshell Labs LLC.

The short version

  • Your chats, voice calls and video calls are end-to-end encrypted. We can't read or listen to them.
  • Your Memories, Library, Reminders, Calendar and Notes are stored on your own computer by default.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

What stays on your device

Memories, photos, audio, notes, reminders, calendar items and Library files you create in the desktop app are saved in a folder on your computer. You can turn on optional encryption (AES-256-GCM) with a passcode and a recovery key. If you lose both, we can't recover that data. Your app-lock PIN and recovery-question answers are stored only on your computer, as one-way hashes; we never receive them and can't read them.

What our servers handle

When you connect to a Cocoshell server, it stores your account details (name, username, email), your encrypted messages and attachments in encrypted form, and the technical records needed to run the service, such as sign-in times, IP addresses and basic logs for security and troubleshooting. Because chats, voice and video are end-to-end encrypted, the server holds only ciphertext and cannot decrypt it.

Personal accounts are hosted on servers operated by Cocoshell and shared with other customers; every account is separate and protected by its own keys. Business accounts run on a private server dedicated to that company.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of your plan and billing contact details, but never your full card number.

Updates and diagnostics

The desktop app checks GitHub for new versions, which means GitHub can see a request from your IP address. We don't include analytics or tracking in the app. If you choose "Contact support" or "Copy diagnostics", technical details such as the app version and operating system are shared only when you send them.

How we use information

Only to run, secure and support the service, process payments, and meet legal obligations. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use your content to train AI models.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate Cocoshell (such as hosting and payment processing), when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users and service. Because of end-to-end encryption, we can't hand over the content of your chats or calls even if asked.

Your choices and rights

You can export or delete your local data at any time from the app. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account information by emailing us, and we'll respond within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your server-side data, subject to short-term backups and legal retention. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, and we honor them.

Retention and security

We keep account data while your account is active and delete it within a reasonable period afterwards. We use encryption in transit and at rest and limit who can access our systems, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children

Cocoshell is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Family plans let a parent or guardian manage accounts for their children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and notify you in the app or by email.
